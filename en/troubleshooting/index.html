About this cheat sheet

This site includes only what happened in practice or what I verified against the official docs, drawn from Chuki DX Lab articles and books. Even commonly heard claims online are left out if I haven't verified them myself. So naturally, some symptoms are not covered here.

The deny rules in your permissions settings (settings.json) are a net that prevents accidental reads and sends, not a wall that stops every method. CLAUDE.md is a set of instructions that Claude reads and tries to follow, and it is not enforced. Many stumbling points come from confusing these two roles.

  • Create the rules you want to block: Permissions Generator
  • Write the assumptions Claude should read every time: CLAUDE.md Template Generator
  • Turn frequent tasks into procedures: Skill (SKILL.md) Scaffold Generator
