How to check Claude Code's terms in an internal review: applicable terms, PDFs, effective dates, and data handling

Published: November 26, 2026 | Chuki DX Lab | Content is based on sources such as the official docs as of writing (November 2026); sources are listed at the end of each section

Key takeaways

  • Which terms apply depends on how you use the product. According to the official docs, Team, Enterprise, and Claude API fall under the Commercial Terms of Service, while Free, Pro, Max, and Ultra fall under the Consumer Terms of Service.
  • The original text is in Anthropic's Legal Center, which has links to the PDF, the Effective date, and the version history. Recording the date and version during your review makes the terms traceable.
  • Check the data training, output rights, and DPA provisions, and have your legal team or the official contact decide how to interpret the terms and whether they apply to your company.

First, determine which terms apply

The terms for Claude Code (an AI coding agent from Anthropic that runs in the terminal) vary by how you use it. According to the official docs, your use is covered by the following terms.

  • Commercial Terms of Service: For Team, Enterprise, and Claude API customers
  • Consumer Terms of Service: for Free, Pro, Max, and Ultra users

Whether you use the Claude API directly or through Amazon Bedrock or Google Cloud's Agent Platform, your existing commercial agreement applies to your use of Claude Code, unless you have separately agreed otherwise with Anthropic.

In a review, first decide how your company uses it (Team or Enterprise, API, via a cloud provider, or an individual plan), then map it to this breakdown.

Source: Legal and compliance

Where to find the original terms, PDFs, and effective dates

According to Anthropic's terms page (Legal Center), the terms and policies are listed together, and each document has a "View more" link and a "PDF" link. The dates are as follows.

  • Consumer Terms of Service (individuals): Oct 8, 2025
  • Commercial Terms of Service (for businesses): Jun 17, 2025
  • Data Processing Addendum (for businesses): Feb 24, 2025
  • Usage Policy (all users): Nov 12, 2026
  • Service Specific Terms (for business): Aug 31, 2026
  • Privacy Policy (consumer): Sep 10, 2026
  • Supported Regions Policy (all users): Nov 12, 2026

The description in the list says the Consumer Terms include using Claude Code on Free, Pro, and Max. The Claude Code official docs also mention Ultra, so if you use Ultra, check the official docs.

When you use Amazon Bedrock, Google Cloud's Vertex, or Microsoft Foundry, the docs say you pay the marketplace provider and that provider's terms also apply. The dates are from the list as of writing (November 2026). Please recheck the original text at the time of your review.

Source: Legal Center

What to check on the Commercial Terms page

The Commercial Terms of Service page shows Download PDF, Effective: Jun 17, 2025, Audience: Business, and Version history.

Contracting parties and formation

The text says that Anthropic is Anthropic Ireland, Limited if the customer resides in the EEA, Switzerland, or the UK, and Anthropic, PBC otherwise. It also says you need legal authority to enter into the agreement on behalf of an organization, and that the terms are not for consumers. The Effective Date is the earlier of the date the customer first agreed electronically and the date they first accessed the service.

Rights in outputs and the DPA

To the extent permitted by applicable law, the customer retains rights to Inputs and owns Outputs. The assignment of rights in Outputs is conditioned on the customer complying with the terms. The text also says Anthropic may not train models on Customer Content. Data is processed under the DPA (the addendum governing processing of personal data), which is incorporated into the terms by reference.

For how to interpret the terms and whether they apply to your company, check with your legal team or the official contact.

Source: Commercial Terms of Service

Data training differs by plan

The official docs state that on Free, Pro, Max, and Ultra, users can choose whether their data is used to improve models, and that when the setting is on, Claude Code usage is also used for training.

For Team, Enterprise, API, third-party platforms, and Claude Gov, Anthropic does not train generative models on the code or prompts you send through Claude Code under the commercial terms. The exception is when a customer chooses to provide data themselves (for example, the Developer Partner Program). An organization admin must explicitly opt in to this program, it covers only Anthropic's first-party API, and Amazon Bedrock and Google Cloud's Agent Platform are not included. Retention periods are covered in a separate guide.

Source: Data usage

Conditions for embedding it in your own products and services

According to the official docs, unless otherwise agreed, preinstalling or running Claude Code in your own products or services (such as hosted sandboxes) requires agreeing to the Commercial Terms of Service and complying with the following conditions.

  • Do not modify the Claude Code binary. Install and run it as Anthropic publishes it, and do not remove, disable, or restrict its built-in authentication methods.
  • Do not pay for Claude usage on behalf of end users, or resell or broker it. Each end user must authenticate with their own API key, Claude subscription credentials, or third-party inference provider credentials.

You may not use the Claude Code or Anthropic names and logos as part of your own product name, feature name, company name, or logo, nor in any way that suggests Anthropic developed, endorses, or is affiliated with your product. Accurately stating in plain text that your product preinstalls or runs Claude Code is permitted.

Source: Legal and compliance

Update notifications and the other materials to review alongside

If you sign up using the form at the bottom of the Legal Center page, you'll get an email when the Commercial Terms of Service, Data Processing Addendum, Usage Policy, Supported Regions Policy, or Service Specific Terms are updated. The Consumer Terms of Service and Privacy Policy are not listed there.

Security certifications and audit reports are collected in the Trust & Compliance Center, which lists items such as SOC 2 Type II. The docs also state that the published Commercial Terms apply uniformly to all business customers and are not negotiable.

Source: Legal Center

FAQ

Which terms apply when I use Claude Code on a Team plan?

According to the Legal Center, the Commercial Terms of Service apply to the API and the Team and Enterprise plans, and the same terms apply to Claude Code used through Team or Enterprise. It states that the DPA is incorporated into the Commercial Terms by reference and applies automatically to business customers.

Source: Legal Center

Where can I find the PDF and version history of the terms?

The Commercial Terms of Service page shows Download PDF, Effective, and Version history.

Source: Commercial Terms of Service

Can contractors and outside vendors use it too?

The Legal Center FAQ says that under the Commercial Terms, a customer can grant access to employees, employees of affiliates, contractors, and other authorized users acting on the customer's behalf, and that the customer is responsible for those users' compliance with the terms and for all activity on the account. For specific cases, check with your legal team or the official contact.

Source: Legal Center
